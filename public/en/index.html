scopes

The complete system.
The scope you need.

Structure only, structure with roofing, or a solution that incorporates cladding and accessories. We define what each proposal includes to make project planning easier.

For turnkey scope, civil works, erection and installations are expressly detailed in the proposal.

  1. Structure onlyColumns, beams, frames, trusses, purlins and bracing.
  2. Structure + roofingAdds the roof and its finishes.
  3. Structure + roofing + claddingIncorporates wall cladding and insulation.
  4. Complete buildingEnvelope and accessories: stairs, platforms and access points according to design.

Each application is developed according to the specifications, loads and scope defined for the supply.
