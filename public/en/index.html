Structural applications

More possibilities to build in steel.

The structural configuration responds to the use of the space, the dimensions and the requirements of each project.

01

Industry and logistics

Production buildings, warehouses, distribution centers, workshops and industrial parks.

02

Commerce and services

Showrooms, commercial buildings, offices and customer service spaces.

03

Large-span spaces

Sports venues, event centers, canopies and hangars, according to span and height requirements.

04

Special structures

Walkways, pipe supports and structures for energy projects, subject to technical evaluation.

Components and configuration

The complete system. The scope you need.

  • Structure. Columns, beams, frames, trusses, purlins and bracing.
  • Envelope. Roofing, cladding, insulation and finishes.
  • Complements. Stairs, platforms, access and components according to design.

From components to an integrated supply

Structure only, structure with roofing, or a solution that includes cladding and complements. We define what each proposal includes to make project planning easier.
